# Commerce Intelligence

## Product
A local-first portfolio analytics workspace for exploring e-commerce funnel progression, repeat-purchase cohorts, category revenue mix, and delivery service levels.

## Audience and task
An analyst or reviewer scans marketplace performance, changes the time window and region, then opens the underlying definitions and data provenance before interpreting a metric.

## Product truth
- The bundled demonstration snapshot is generated synthetic data shaped like a Brazilian marketplace. It is not the original public Olist dataset or verified business performance.
- Monetary values are Brazilian reais (BRL).
- GMV is item price plus freight for orders outside canceled and unavailable states.
- Cohort retention uses distinct customers who purchased again in each calendar month divided by distinct customers in their first eligible purchase month.
- Late delivery compares delivered customer timestamp with estimated delivery timestamp, counting each order once.
- Scenario controls are illustrative and never describe a realized operational improvement.
- No credentials, external services, or network-backed data source are required.
